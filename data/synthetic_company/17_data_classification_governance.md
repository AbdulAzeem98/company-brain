# Enterprise Data Classification & Governance Policy

> **NOTICE: SYNTHETIC DATASET**
> *This document is part of a synthetic, fictional dataset created for Nexora Technologies. All names, projects, policies, metrics, and details are purely fictional.*

*Classification: Manager & Leadership Access Only*

This policy governs the formal data classification tiers, handling protocols, and retention requirements across Nexora Technologies systems and repositories.

## Data Classification Tiers
1. **Public Tier**: Marketing collateral, open-source code repositories, public documentation, and press announcements.
2. **Internal / Employee Tier**: Standard operational handbooks, engineering guidelines, benefit summaries, and all-hands presentation recordings.
3. **Confidential / Manager Tier**: Departmental budget breakdowns, performance calibrations, sales pipeline forecasts, and technical program status reports.
4. **Restricted Tier (HR / Finance / Executive)**:
   - Executive M&A negotiations and valuation targets.
   - Bank account details, wire instructions, and capitalization tables.
   - Individual compensation, equity grants, and formal workplace grievance records.
   - Customer personally identifiable information (PII) subject to GDPR/CCPA.

## Access Governance & Enforcement
- Access to Restricted Tier assets requires role-based clearance provisioned through Okta Identity Governance with mandatory quarterly access certifications.
- Exporting, printing, or transcribing Restricted Tier records outside approved enterprise vaults is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate disciplinary review.
