## Delegation of Authority & Approval Thresholds
- **Tier 1 (Up to $5,000 USD)**: Department Manager approval. Includes standard SaaS subscriptions, individual team offsites, and specialized hardware peripherals.
- **Tier 2 ($5,001 to $25,000 USD)**: Functional Vice President sign-off. Includes multi-seat software licenses, localized consulting engagements, and marketing sponsorships.
- **Tier 3 ($25,001 to $100,000 USD)**: CFO (Evelyn Reed) approval required. Requires three competing vendor bids and standard vendor risk assessment.
- **Tier 4 (Above $100,000 USD)**: Joint approval by CEO and CFO, plus Board Audit Committee notification for capital commitments exceeding 24 months.

## Travel & Corporate Card Reconciliation
- All employee corporate card charges via Ramp must be substantiated with itemized receipts uploaded within **7 business days** of transaction occurrence.
- Flight bookings must be economy class for domestic travel under 5 flight hours; business class is permitted for international transoceanic flights exceeding 7 hours.
- Per Diem Meals: $85/day domestic US; $120/day international Tier-1 cities (London, Tokyo, Zurich).
- Unreconciled card balances older than 30 days are subject to automatic suspension and payroll escalation.
