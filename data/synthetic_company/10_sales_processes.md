# Enterprise Sales Process & Playbook

> **NOTICE: SYNTHETIC DATASET**
> *This document is part of a synthetic, fictional dataset created for Nexora Technologies. All names, projects, policies, metrics, and details are purely fictional.*

This playbook outlines the standard pipeline stages, qualification criteria (MEDDPICC), and discounting authorization matrices for Nexora Enterprise Sales.

## Pipeline Stages & Criteria
1. **Stage 1: Discovery & Qualification (10%)**: Identified economic buyer, pain points mapped to Nexora Pulse capabilities, MEDDPICC score recorded in Salesforce.
2. **Stage 2: Technical Validation / POC (30%)**: 14-day timeboxed Proof of Concept running Pulse Collector Daemon on a designated staging cluster (min. 50 nodes). Success criteria pre-signed by VP Infrastructure.
3. **Stage 3: Business Value Alignment & Proposal (60%)**: Formal ROI calculation showing at least 3.2x TCO reduction relative to legacy observability suites (Datadog/Dynatrace).
4. **Stage 4: Legal & Security Review (80%)**: MSA, SOC 2 Type II audit report review, DPA (Data Processing Agreement) and InfoSec questionnaire clearance.
5. **Stage 5: Negotiation & Close (90%)**: Final pricing approval and DocuSign delivery.
6. **Closed-Won (100%)**: Executed contracts transferred to Customer Engineering for onboarding.

## Discount Authority Matrix
- **Up to 10% Discount**: Account Executive (AE) discretion for multi-year prepaid contracts.
- **11% to 20% Discount**: Requires written approval from Regional VP of Sales.
- **21% to 35% Discount**: Requires joint sign-off from Chief Revenue Officer (Jackson Briggs) and VP Finance.
- **Above 35% Discount**: Prohibited without formal sign-off from CEO (Dr. Aris Thorne) and requires mandatory 3-year term commitment.
