## Compensation Philosophy & Benchmarking
Nexora targets the **75th percentile of Radford Global Technology Survey benchmarks** for base salaries and equity grants across engineering, product, and enterprise go-to-market positions.

- **Leveling Matrix**:
  - L3 (Associate Engineer / Specialist): Base $110,000 - $140,000 | Equity: 10,000 options (4-year vesting, 1-year cliff)
  - L4 (Senior Engineer / Senior Specialist): Base $145,000 - $185,000 | Equity: 25,000 options
  - L5 (Staff Engineer / Principal / Manager): Base $190,000 - $240,000 | Equity: 55,000 options
  - L6 (Senior Staff / Director): Base $245,000 - $310,000 | Equity: 110,000 options
- **Annual Merit Cycle**: Performance appraisals are conducted in October with compensation adjustments effective December 1. Discretionary performance bonuses range from 0% to 25% of base salary.

## Workplace Dispute & Grievance Resolution
- All allegations of harassment, discrimination, or ethical misconduct must be immediately routed to the Head of People Operations and external counsel within 24 hours of report receipt.
- An independent fact-finding investigator is appointed within 3 business days.
- Retaliation against any employee reporting a grievance or participating in an investigation is grounds for immediate termination of employment.
