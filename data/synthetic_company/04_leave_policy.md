# Leave & Time Off Policy

> **NOTICE: SYNTHETIC DATASET**
> *This document is part of a synthetic, fictional dataset created for Nexora Technologies. All names, projects, policies, metrics, and details are purely fictional.*

Nexora Technologies believes that rest, family time, and health recuperation are essential to sustainable high performance.

## Paid Time Off (PTO) Framework
- **Flexible / Unlimited PTO**: Salaried full-time exempt US employees operate on an honor-based flexible PTO model. We mandate a **minimum of 20 days off per year** to prevent burnout.
- **Accrual Basis (Non-US & Non-Exempt)**: Employees governed by regional labor statutes accrue 2.08 days per month (25 days annually), plus statutory bank and public holidays.
- **Blackout Periods**: There are no company-wide blackout windows, except that critical on-call rotations for SREs during major infrastructure upgrades require 3 weeks advance shift-swapping.

## Parental Leave
- **Primary & Secondary Caregiver Leave**: All new parents—regardless of gender, birth, adoption, or surrogacy—are eligible for **16 weeks of 100% paid parental leave**.
- **Timing**: Parental leave must be taken within the first 12 months following birth or placement. It may be taken continuously or in two separate blocks with manager approval.

## Medical & Bereavement Leave
- **Short-Term Disability / Sick Days**: Up to 10 consecutive working days of sick leave are self-certified without requiring a physician's note. Absences exceeding 10 consecutive business days trigger our Short-Term Disability insurance plan (paying 80% base salary for up to 180 days).
- **Bereavement Leave**: 10 paid consecutive days for immediate family members; 5 paid consecutive days for extended family.
- **Mental Health Days**: "Nexora Recharge Days" are designated quarterly company-wide shutdown days (one Friday per quarter).
