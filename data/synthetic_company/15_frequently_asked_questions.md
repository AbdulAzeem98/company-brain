# Nexora Technologies — Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

> **NOTICE: SYNTHETIC DATASET**
> *This document is part of a synthetic, fictional dataset created for Nexora Technologies. All names, projects, policies, metrics, and details are purely fictional.*

Here are answers to the most common questions asked by Nexora team members across IT, HR, engineering, and office operations.

## General Operations & Workplace
### Q: How do I request new software licenses or development tools?
**A:** Submit a ticket through the IT Service Desk in Slack using the `/request-software` shortcut. Tools under $100/month (e.g. JetBrains, Figma) are auto-approved by your direct manager; enterprise tools require IT Security review.

### Q: What is our policy on using external AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini)?
**A:** You may use enterprise-contracted AI tools provisioned through Nexora SSO (such as Company Brain or our licensed AI workspace). Never paste customer telemetry data, proprietary source code, or unredacted PII into unapproved public AI consumer interfaces.

### Q: What days are company holidays?
**A:** Nexora observes all standard US federal holidays plus 4 quarterly "Recharge Days". For international team members, local statutory public holidays are honored according to your regional employment agreement.

## Payroll & Benefits
### Q: When does payroll run?
**A:** US payroll is processed semi-monthly on the 15th and the last business day of each month. International entities process on the 25th of each month.

### Q: How do I submit home internet or office equipment expenses?
**A:** Use the Ramp mobile or web app. Connect your personal payment card or use your virtual Nexora Ramp card. Tag the expense category as "Remote Work Connectivity" or "Home Office Stipend" and attach a receipt photo.

## Engineering & Telemetry Platform
### Q: Where do I find the staging credentials for Nexora Pulse?
**A:** Credentials and test tokens are stored in the engineering 1Password vault under `Staging-Pulse-Sandbox`. Access is granted to all full-time engineering and product personnel upon completing onboarding.
