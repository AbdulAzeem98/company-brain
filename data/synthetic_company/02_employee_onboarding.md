# New Hire Onboarding Guide

> **NOTICE: SYNTHETIC DATASET**
> *This document is part of a synthetic, fictional dataset created for Nexora Technologies. All names, projects, policies, metrics, and details are purely fictional.*

Welcome to Nexora Technologies! This guide outlines the essential steps, systems access, and milestones to help you integrate seamlessly during your first 90 days.

## Week 1: Foundation & Setup
- **Workstation & Identity**: Complete Okta SSO activation, set up hardware-backed FIDO2 multi-factor authentication (YubiKey), and configure your 1Password enterprise vault.
- **Benefits Enrollment**: Review health, dental, vision, and 401(k) matching plans in the Nexora People Portal within 14 calendar days of start date.
- **Slack Channels**: Join `#announcements`, `#watercooler`, `#help-it`, `#people-ops`, and your dedicated department channel.
- **Onboarding Buddy**: You have been assigned an onboarding buddy from a peer team who will schedule daily 15-minute check-ins during Week 1.

## 30-Day Milestone: Context & Integration
- Complete all mandatory compliance modules: Data Privacy & GDPR/CCPA, Harassment Prevention, and SOC 2 Type II Security Awareness.
- Review the Nexora Architecture Guide and set up local development or sales enablement environments.
- Conduct introductory 1-on-1s with key cross-functional stakeholders identified by your manager.

## 60-Day Milestone: Autonomy & Contribution
- Deliver your first major work item:
  - For Engineering: Ship code to production or complete an internal RFC review.
  - For GTM: Lead your first prospective customer demo or customer journey mapping session.
  - For Operations: Audit or optimize an existing workflow or standard operating procedure.
- Gather midpoint 360-degree feedback with your manager to calibrate expectations.

## 90-Day Milestone: Full Ownership
- Fully assume independent operational responsibility for your primary functional domain.
- Present a "Fresh Eyes" review to your department head highlighting areas of improvement observed during onboarding.
- Formulate your quarterly OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) aligned with annual company targets.
