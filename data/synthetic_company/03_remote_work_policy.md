# Remote Work & Hybrid Workplace Policy

> **NOTICE: SYNTHETIC DATASET**
> *This document is part of a synthetic, fictional dataset created for Nexora Technologies. All names, projects, policies, metrics, and details are purely fictional.*

Nexora Technologies embraces a "Digital-First" workplace strategy designed to afford employees geographical flexibility while sustaining teamwork and rigorous operational security.

## Core Principles & Working Hours
- **Core Collaboration Hours**: All team members are expected to be available for synchronous collaboration during our core company window: **10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their designated primary regional time zone** (US Eastern, US Pacific, or Central European Time).
- **Asynchronous Communication**: Detailed written documentation via Confluence/Notion, pull requests, and recorded Loom updates takes precedence over spontaneous meetings.

## Home Office Setup & Equipment
Every full-time employee receives:
- **Company Hardware**: One standardized enterprise MacBook Pro (M3 Max / 64GB) or Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon with pre-installed Jamf/InTune MDM agents and CrowdStrike Falcon EDR.
- **Initial Home Office Setup Stipend**: A one-time, non-taxable stipend of **$1,500 USD** (or local currency equivalent) to purchase ergonomic desks, chairs, external monitors, or peripherals via the Ramp expense portal within the first 60 days.
- **Monthly Connectivity Allowance**: **$100 USD/month** auto-reimbursed on payroll for high-speed fiber internet and mobile data plans.

## Work from Anywhere (WFA) International Travel
- Employees may work from an international location other than their tax residence for up to **30 business days per rolling 12-month calendar year**, provided they obtain prior approval from their People Partner and Manager at least 14 days in advance to ensure compliance with international tax and export control treaties.
- High-risk jurisdictions designated under US Department of State Level 3 or 4 travel advisories are strictly prohibited for remote access.
